# Trueup — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Transformation and PMO lead

- As a transformation lead, I want one portfolio view that separates announced, gated, attested and written-down value, so that I can answer the board without rebuilding a spreadsheet every quarter.
- As a transformation lead, I want each initiative classified on process, data and workforce, so that I can see how much of my portfolio is genuine reinvention rather than automation wearing a new label.
- As a transformation lead, I want the scale gate to hold a pilot out of enterprise reporting until its effect replicates outside the pilot unit, so that small-scale wins stop inflating the portfolio number.

### Initiative business owner

- As a service-line medical director, I want to lock my baseline and measurement method before the pilot starts, so that the result is not renegotiated once the number becomes inconvenient.
- As a revenue-cycle director, I want to see realised value posted against my own operating budget line, so that the saving lands where I am actually measured.
- As an initiative owner, I want to write down unrealised benefit on schedule with a stated reason and no escalation, so that telling the truth early costs less than defending a number I no longer believe. *(exception path)*
- As an initiative owner, I want a drift alert when my live process stops performing, so that I find out before the saving quietly disappears from next year's budget.

### Finance business partner and internal audit

- As a finance business partner, I want to attest or reject realised value against the ledger, so that nothing reaches the board that I cannot tie to a ledger line or an actuarial trend.
- As an internal auditor, I want the complete evidence chain for any attested benefit — baseline, method, cohort, gate decisions and approvers — so that an audit finding does not require reconstructing two years from email.
- As a finance business partner, I want claimed and attested value held structurally apart and never summed, so that optimism cannot be laundered into the forecast. *(governance path)*

### Algorithmic accountability committee chair

- As the committee chair, I want an initiative's value gate held closed until subgroup impact is measured, so that fairness review has consequences rather than an advisory footnote. *(governance path)*
- As the committee chair, I want utilisation-management and prior-authorisation initiatives reported with denial and appeal-overturn rates beside their savings, so that we can see when a saving is regulatory exposure in disguise.
- As the committee chair, I want benefit accrual suspended when a clinical model's validation expires, so that value cannot keep accumulating against a stale claim.

### Workforce planning lead

- As a workforce planning lead, I want each initiative's redesigned roles, redeployment plan and required skills approved before go-live, so that role change is planned rather than announced.
- As a workforce planning lead, I want benefits that depend on a single external hire flagged as at-risk until capability transfer completes, so that buying 82 percent of our capability from outside does not mean 82 percent single points of failure.
